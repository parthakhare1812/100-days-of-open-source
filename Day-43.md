# Day 43 - Road Quality Analytics Case Study

## Objective

Create a real-world case study demonstrating automated road condition assessment.

## Scenario

Urban road with potholes and surface damage requiring maintenance planning.

## Workflow

1. Road Image Upload
2. Image Processing
3. Defect Detection
4. Road Assessment
5. Severity Scoring
6. Maintenance Recommendation

## Deliverables

- Case Study
- Workflow Diagram

## Learnings

- Computer vision can automate infrastructure inspections.
- Severity scoring helps prioritize repairs.
- Analytics improves maintenance decision-making.
