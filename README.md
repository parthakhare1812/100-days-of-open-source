# 📖 Overview

DriveLegal is a next-generation Transportation Intelligence Platform that leverages Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, OCR, Cloud Computing, and DevOps to modernize transportation operations.

The platform helps transportation authorities, road agencies, emergency services, and smart city initiatives improve:

* Vehicle Verification
* Fraud Detection
* Crash Severity Prediction
* Road Infrastructure Monitoring
* Transportation Analytics
* Governance & Compliance

DriveLegal is designed as a scalable, cloud-native, enterprise-grade ecosystem combining technology, governance, and business strategy into a unified solution.

To create safer roads, smarter transportation systems, and data-driven governance through intelligent automation and analytics.

# 🚨 Problem Statement

Transportation ecosystems continue to face challenges such as:

* Manual document verification
* Fraudulent vehicle records
* Delayed accident assessment
* Poor road condition visibility
* Inefficient infrastructure maintenance
* Fragmented transportation systems
* Lack of predictive intelligence

These challenges lead to:

* Increased operational costs
* Reduced efficiency
* Public safety risks
* Delayed decision-making
