# Day 45 - Enterprise Deployment Case Study

## Objective

Demonstrate how DriveLegal can scale to support enterprise and government deployments.

## Deployment Scope

- Multi-City
- State-Wide
- Thousands of Users
- Millions of Records

## Features Used

- Vehicle Verification
- Crash Severity Prediction
- Road Quality Analytics
- Centralized Reporting

## Deliverables

- Enterprise Case Study
- Enterprise Deployment Diagram

## Learnings

- Scalability is a critical design consideration.
- Enterprise platforms require centralized governance.
- AI systems can support large-scale public infrastructure programs.
